Why this one holds up

What it gets right

  • Photos still win. Colour saturates on dark, so her nail work is the brightest thing on every screen.
  • Colour does the navigating. One colour per service means a returning client never reads a menu twice.
  • It reads young without reading cheap. That is the exact gap between 01 and 02.
  • Every effect here is CSS. Glows, gradients and the ticker cost nothing and need no library.
  • It matches a phone at night, which is when it will actually be opened.

What it will cost you

  • Contrast needs watching. Orange and mint on dark are fine at size, risky for small grey-on-dark text. The body colour is deliberately light for that reason.
  • Four colours is a discipline. They only ever mean nails, lashes, makeup and confirmation. The moment they become decoration it turns into a circus.
  • Her photos need dark-friendly backgrounds. Worth telling her before she shoots, not after.
  • Still a bet on photography. Less brutal than pure black, but the same bet.

This is a sketch, not the answer. Show it to Dani next to A and B and let her push it. More pink, less purple, rounder, harder, whatever she says. When it settles, the whole thing collapses into one prototypes/theme.css, and that file is what ports into globals.css the first time real UI gets built.
