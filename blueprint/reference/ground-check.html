How light is "a tiny lighter"?

Same hero four times. Only the ground, the card colour and the photo placeholder move; the pink, orange, violet and type are identical in all four. Pick a number and that becomes --ground in the theme.

The surround is neutral grey on purpose. Judging a near-black against a purple page lies to you.

The tradeoff, said plainly. Every step lighter costs the neon some punch, because the accents only glow against something near-black. Step 3 is in here as the wall, not as a candidate. It is the point where the ground stops being a backdrop and starts being a purple you have to design around.

Step 1 is where I would land. It takes the severity off without giving anything real away, and it is kinder to her photos, since a shot with a slightly lifted background will not leave a visible rectangle floating on the page.

Say a number and it goes straight into prototypes/theme.css as --ground, with the card colour moving with it.
