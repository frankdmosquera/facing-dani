After Dark

Near-black ground with two loud accents. Nail photography glows against black the way it does on a phone screen at night, and the whole thing reads expensive without costing anything to build.

Palette

#0B0B0Fground
#1A1922cards
#FF2D78accent
#C8FF3Dpop
#F5F4F7text

Type

Antondisplay, caps, tight
Intereverything else

What makes it work

  • Black hides a weak photo. Chrome, glitter and neon sets look twice as good on it.
  • Two accents is a whole design system. Magenta for actions, lime for one highlight per page.
  • Streetwear and concert-poster language, which is native to her age group.
  • Cheapest to execute well. Type and one photo per section, no decoration.
Watch out for Dark sites punish bad photography badly. Every shot needs to be sharp and well lit or the whole page looks cheap. This direction is a bet on her photos being good.

Sugar Rush

White ground, blocks of saturated candy colour, chunky rounded type and a scrolling ticker. The friendliest of the three and the closest to how her clients already decorate their own feeds.

Palette

#FFFDF8ground
#FF4D9Dprimary
#FF7A2Fsecond
#9B6BFFthird
#3DDCA0fourth
#21102Bink

Type

Fredokadisplay, rounded, warm
Interbody and prices

What makes it work

  • Colour blocks do the structuring, so sections divide themselves without you laying anything out.
  • A four-colour system means each service gets its own colour. Nails pink, lashes purple, makeup orange.
  • Approachable and unintimidating, which matters when a 15-year-old is booking her first set.
  • Survives mediocre photos better than black does.
Watch out for The closest of the three to looking childish if the colours drift pale. Keep them saturated. Pastel is where this direction goes to die.

Chrome

Iridescent and metallic gradients, tight modern grotesk, one hot accent. Chrome is one of the biggest things in nails right now, so the site ends up looking like the work she is selling.

Palette

#F2F1F4ground
iridescentgradient
#17161Cink
#FF2D78accent

Type

Space Groteskdisplay and body, one family

What makes it work

  • The one direction here that no other Calgary salon will be running.
  • The gradient is pure CSS, so it costs nothing and never loads slowly.
  • One typeface across the whole site removes a decision you would otherwise have to keep making.
  • Ages into a rebrand easily. Swap the accent and it is a different brand.
Watch out for Trend-dated fastest of the three. Chrome is hot now; in three years it reads as 2026. Fine if the site gets refreshed, a problem if it has to last.

My read

01 After Dark if her photos are good, because it is the one that looks most like money and it is the least work to keep looking right. 02 Sugar Rush if she wants to feel welcoming to girls her own age and younger, and if the photo quality is going to be uneven. 03 Chrome if being unmistakably different in Calgary matters more than lasting five years.

None of these are locked. They exist so you can point at one and say more of that, less of this. The output of that conversation is a single prototypes/theme.css, which is the file that ports into globals.css when the first real UI feature gets built.

Still open

  1. Her brand name. Is Facing Dani the real name or a placeholder?
  2. A logo. If one exists the palette gets sampled from it and two of these directions change.
  3. Where she works. Home, a rented chair, or mobile. It decides what goes in the schema, and it is a privacy question at 17.
  4. How she takes bookings. Instagram DM is the honest answer for this clientele.
